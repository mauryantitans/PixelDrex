WHY IT WORKS
THE WORLD LEARNS PHYSICS
ONE SITUATION, ONE QUESTION
Cells in the same situation share one Drex question, and answers are cached across steps — 41,984 cell decisions took only 1,832 questions. Drex's answers become the world's physics.
PROBABILITY IS THE DICE
The next state is sampled from Drex's calibrated probabilities, so “lightning rarely strikes” and “ash slowly regrows” really are rare and slow.
“a zombie outbreak in a city” — the infection spreads in glowing waves and the city turns over. 41,984 cell decisions from 1,350 questions, 26 s, $0.012.
