WHY IT WORKS
THE WORLD LEARNS PHYSICS
ONE SITUATION, ONE QUESTION
Cells in the same situation share one Drex question, and answers are cached across steps. By the end of the forest fire, each step needed about one new question.
PROBABILITY IS THE DICE
The next state is sampled from Drex's calibrated probabilities, so “lightning rarely strikes” and “ash slowly regrows” really are rare and slow.
“a zombie outbreak in a city” — the city grows, zombies creep in from the corners and take it. 40,960 cell decisions from 590 questions, 17 s, $0.008.
